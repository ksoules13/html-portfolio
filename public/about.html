About Me

Hello there!

My name is Kai! Currently I reside in a small town in Northern Arizona and work full time as a Paramedic with a private ambulance company. My wife and I have plans to move to Washington state later in 2030. We’ve been together for the past 6 years and married for 5 of those years. We met through work at our local hospital and have been inseparable since we’ve met. Over the past 6 years we have gathered a zoo of animals since my wife has a bleeding heart and feels the need to take home every stray she falls for. Our current count is 6 cats, 2 dogs, a sulcata tortious and 28+ chickens.

I am a human of many interests. I enjoy outdoor activities like kayaking, gardening, hiking and tending to my chickens. However, when I’m not outside, I’m playing games, playing around with graphic designs, codes or 3d printing various items. One day I’d love to be able to aid in the advances of patient care in the field.

As for coding and graphic design, I spent a lot of my time dabbling as a teenager. Over the years I stopped since my family always told me the medical field was more important. However, overtime technology has become such a large part of everybody’s lives. So, I decided to get back into it and have taught myself many of the things I know. I’m one day to make a shift from full time paramedic to a full time web and graphic designer. .
